# Inbox Flight Deck

A local inbox triage agent for independent consultants who lose next actions in client messages.

## The Unique Kernel
Every proposed action includes an exact source quote. A plan is only a proposal: inspect, select, apply to the local board, and undo. The visible before/after turns an opaque AI answer into an inspectable workflow.

## Who It's For
A solo consultant juggling client deadlines, unanswered requests, and newsletters. Today they reread a mixed inbox and manually copy tasks to a list.

## The Core Loop
Load a clearly fictional inbox or paste messages → request a triage plan → inspect priority, action, rationale and evidence → select decisions → apply to a local board → export the action brief or undo.

## Inspiration & Identity
Agent-selected direction under delegated authority: calm editorial operations desk, warm paper, dark ink, orange highlights, compact inbox rows, strong typography and clear status indicators.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The owner requested a memorable, working one-day hackathon build using TypeScript/Node and an AI agent. Personal motivations and learning outcomes were not supplied.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A reviewer can see eight mixed messages become a prioritized plan, open the evidence behind an urgent decision, exclude a proposal, apply the rest, export a Markdown brief, and undo in under two minutes. Real Gemini inference is an explicit mode; a labeled deterministic rehearsal mode works without credentials.

## The POC Boundary
One browser surface, local board, text input, sample messages, bounded LLM planning, evidence validation, human-selected application, undo and export. No external inbox mutation.

## Later
OAuth inbox connectors, calendar integration, persistent accounts, and provider adapters.

## Explicitly Cut
- Sending replies or deleting mail: unnecessary to demonstrate triage and would add external side effects.
- Filesystem organizer: real email evidence is easier to inspect on video than moving personal files.
- Deployment and authentication: local recording and a public source repository satisfy the submission format.

## Workflow provenance
Created before implementation using Devpost Learn `1-start` and `2-scope` and their templates. Interview/sign-off pacing is adapted to the owner's explicit delegation; these are agent-selected decisions, not invented learner answers.
